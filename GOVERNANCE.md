# Governance

DirectiveGraph uses maintainer-led governance while the project is small.

- Akhilesh Gogikar is the initial maintainer and final decision-maker.
- Routine changes are decided through public pull-request review when the repository is public.
- Decisions prioritize documented discovery semantics, conservative claims, deterministic output, offline operation, accessibility, and [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md).
- A new format or precedence relation requires primary-source provenance, fixtures, tests, and a changelog entry. Security fixes may be prepared privately.
- Repeated, sustained contributors may be invited as reviewers or maintainers. Repository administration, release, and security permissions are granted separately and at least privilege.

If the maintainer is unavailable, the project remains usable under MIT; no continuity or release cadence is guaranteed.
